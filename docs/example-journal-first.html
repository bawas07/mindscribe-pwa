00 — Onboarding
mind·scribe
A quiet place to write, that writes back.
What it is

A private journal. Every word stays on this device — nothing is sent anywhere. When you're stuck, it offers a gentle nudge; over time, it remembers your patterns so it can meet you where you are.

What it isn't

Therapy, or a diagnosis. MindScribe is a writing companion, not a clinician — and not a replacement for real support. If it ever sounds like you might be in crisis, it will quietly point you to resources that can actually help.
